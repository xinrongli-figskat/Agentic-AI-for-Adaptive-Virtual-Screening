## Interpretation and next boundary

The strong depth–label association is a major selection/measurement limitation. It is not an oracle leak because auxiliary columns are absent from the public pool, but TNAP should **not** be described as a uniformly measured screen or as evidence of direct protein binding. The construct is unspecified. The large fixed-greedy and forced-cold-start effects are observations in this single `limited` pool, not proof of general advantage or an independent GLM gain. D4 would require a separately approved GLM arm and should examine the measurement-depth confounding before claiming broader value.

No original DYRK3 file, original D3 failed assertion, v2 failed assertion, or frozen TNAP file was rewritten. Hard-stop trigger at the end of D3: **none**. Outstanding scientific decision: whether the `limited` TNAP pool is acceptable for a later GLM comparison, given the depth–label association; D3 CPU findings do not settle that decision.
